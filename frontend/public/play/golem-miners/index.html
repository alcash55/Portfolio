Golem Miners

The browser build is single-player. It is about 62 MB, and the world takes a few seconds to generate once it loads.

Loading the game

W A S D
Move. Shift sprints, Space jumps
Mouse
Look. Hold left click to chop or dig
E
Pick up, talk, craft at the workbench
Tab
Bag and skills
1 to 6
Hotbar slot
Esc
Settings, and frees the mouse

Start by punching a tree for logs. Logs and flint make a pickaxe at the camp workbench.
